# Evidence JSON for `render_report.py`

All prose is plain text; the script escapes LaTeX punctuation. Required keys:

- `lab`, `institution`, `method_notes`, `alumni_scope`: strings.
- `worked_example`: boolean. If false, require `eligible: true` after checking all five eligibility conditions **outside** this JSON, including the ten-week history. Do not serialize or print the ten-week check.
- `criteria`: exactly four objects `{ "label": "Disease context", "score": 4 }` in this order: disease context, wet/dry integration, active independent lab, overseas-trained first-author trainee. Score evidence strength from 1 to 5 stars. Explain material uncertainty in the relevant prose; no footnotes or notes under the criteria table.
- `institute_summary`: object `{ "text": "Institutional setting and relevant core facilities.", "refs": ["18", "19"] }`, placed after Criteria.
- `working_language`: object `{ "text": "Best estimate: English is the lab's common working language, based on ...", "refs": ["20", "3"] }`. Keep it to one or two sentences after Institute; cite the basis and signal that an inference is an estimate.
- `rie2030_alignment`: object `{ "text": "Specific RIE2030 thrust and matching work, with the translation or Singapore-specific evidence gap.", "refs": ["A", "21"] }`. Put this section after Postdoc fit on page 1; cite the original lab paper(s) and the official NRF factsheet. Add `https://file.go.gov.sg/rie2030factsheet.pdf` to `other_references`. A plausible transferable skill is not a demonstrated deployment or funding commitment.
- `research_directions`: exactly two objects `{ "source_type": "stated", "text": "PI profile's published research directions", "refs": ["1"] }` and `{ "source_type": "inferred", "text": "Direction inferred from recent papers", "refs": ["A", "B"] }`.
- `ownership`: one or two short objects `{ "lead": "Trainee-led mechanism.", "text": "Finding, PI senior role and trainee ownership; why this matters to a postdoc.", "refs": ["B", "1"] }`. Avoid repeating research topics, method counts or full alumni destinations.
- `fit`: 3–4 concise objects `{ "label": "Skill gain", "text": "Specific marginal training gain for this applicant.", "refs": ["A"] }`. Other labels may address clinical link, career geography and PI context. The renderer uses flowing paragraphs so the left column fills before the right.
- `alumni`: one object per verifiable named **PhD-qualified** alumnus with lab service in the five-year window: `{ "name": "X", "connection": "Former postdoc in this lab; 2022–24", "outcome": "Current role, or appointment unverified", "refs": ["5"] }`. Put former postdocs and fellows first. Distinguish PhD graduates without subsequent postdoc service in `connection`. Never present a publicity count as a complete alumni roster.
- `postdoc_examples`: 2–4 concise page-1 former-fellow outcomes `{ "name": "X", "text": "Former fellow; now an independent PI at...", "refs": ["5"] }`. Use an empty list only if no former PhD-qualified fellow trajectory can be verified. Keep role and first-author detail in `ownership` or the fuller page-2 roster.
- `papers`: 5–8 original-paper objects `{ "key": "A", "first_author": "Ahn", "journal": "Cell", "year": 2023, "topic": "bat ASC2 and inflammasomes", "last_author": "Lin-Fa Wang", "senior_role": "PI corresponding; co-led with trainee", "doi": "10.1016/j.cell.2023.03.036" }`. Include every selected primary study only once; codes are unique. If the last author is a collaborator, state that explicitly and describe the target PI's actual role. The generator will print both.
- `methods`: objects `{ "name": "Flow cytometry or sorting", "papers": ["B", "D"], "example": "Bat leukocyte phenotyping and donor B-cell sorting; platform access unconfirmed." }`. The script validates codes and computes counts. Define families by concrete skill, not by disease topic. Only map papers whose methods section supports the technique. Recurrent = two or more papers.
- `other_references`: short official-source objects `{ "key": "1", "label": "Official PI profile", "reason": "Current role and research", "url": "https://..." }`. Source numbers may appear in `refs`.
- `figures`: optional objects `{ "key": "A", "pdf": "source-pdfs/A.pdf", "page": 3, "label": "Ahn et al. 2023" }`. Paths are relative to the JSON file; `page` is one-based. Identify the correct source page by rendering or text extraction first. The output PDF bookmarks carry paper key and source page.

The report has no top decision banner, date, geographic tag, printed ten-week metric, or asterisk footnote in `Criteria`. A worked example's otherwise-ineligible geography may be mentioned in the applicant-fit prose if relevant. Keep page 1 concise, or compilation fails its two-page pre-append check. Its starred multicolumn environment reads to the bottom of the left column before the right; reserve enough space to avoid orphaned section headings. For an unfamiliar applicant, replace the default skill baseline with facts supplied by the user.
