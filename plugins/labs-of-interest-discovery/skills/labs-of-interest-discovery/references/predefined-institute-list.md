## Recurring identity traps

- “German Cancer Consortium (DKTK), partner site X, German Cancer Research Center” often describes an external partner site's affiliation, not a lab at DKFZ. A DKFZ Clinical Cooperation Unit in Heidelberg may be genuine; verify case by case.
- “Institut Pasteur” can mean Paris, Lille, Dakar, Tunis, New Caledonia, or other locations. Require the Paris institute.
- “Institute of Cancer Research” without “London” can identify institutes elsewhere, including China. Likewise, “Institute of Medical Science” alone does not establish IMSUT.
- “RIKEN” alone does not establish IMS or BDR, and “Max Planck Institute” alone does not identify either named Max Planck entry.
- A cancer hospital and its affiliated research institute can be separate entities. Verify the senior investigator's actual research group instead of granting a hospital affiliation automatically.
- Do not add Singapore institutions. If the same author genuinely runs an active lab at a named institute on the predefined list and also has an affiliation in another country, verify the predefined-list appointment rather than accepting or rejecting on the additional address alone.
